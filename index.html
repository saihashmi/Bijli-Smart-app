From Clinical Insight to Operational Reality

Health insurance runs on decisions that are part clinical, part operational and part data. I work where those meet.

I'm a medical doctor (MBBS, 2004) and Manager Medical Services at EFU Life Assurance. My team handles hospital pre-authorization and case management, deciding on clinical necessity, length of stay and policy coverage, and keeps the 24/7 Medical Approval Centre answering around the clock.

Faster answers for patients and hospitals, with doctors still accountable for clinical decisions.

Alongside the job I make the process measurable: daily case-trend reports, turnaround-time dashboards by time band and region, a system gap analysis to automate the pre-authorization form, and AI integration with the health services app team. I also build small web tools to test ideas; seven of them are live below.
